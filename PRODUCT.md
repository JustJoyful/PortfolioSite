# Product

## What it is

The personal portfolio of Joydeep Das, a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Netaji Subhash Engineering College, Kolkata. One page: hero, About, Work, Contact.

## Who it is for

People hiring for AI/ML engineering roles and people offering research positions, equally. About keeps shipped systems and controlled experiments side by side.

## What the visitor should do

See the work. The first screen says what Joydeep does ("AI engineer building local LLM agents, retrieval and speech pipelines, and running controlled ML experiments.") and its one primary button scrolls to the Work gallery. Email is the contact route.

## Mode

Experience: the work leads. The generative peony is the site's signature and stays; in Work the projects take the room and the flower dims.

## Content truth

- Projects: AxiomLM, Testcard, S.P.E.A.K., VGC-Telemetry, each with real screenshots, charts and a demo recording (`media/` sources, encoded copies in `public/work/`).
- All copy lives in `src/data/content.js`. No invented proof: no testimonials, logos or metrics beyond the projects' own results.

## Constraints

- React 19, Vite, Tailwind 3; the WebGL bloom engine in `src/engine/`.
- WCAG AA contrast, 44 px targets, reduced motion and Save-Data respected, CSP `default-src 'self'`.
